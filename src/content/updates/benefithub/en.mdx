Tuggi is now part of BenefitHub, a benefits platform that companies in Brazil offer to their employees. People who have BenefitHub through work can already find Tuggi there, next to travel brands such as Wyndham and Days Inn.

<ArticleFigure src="/images/updates/benefithub/benefithub-vitrine.jpg" alt="Tuggi on the BenefitHub storefront, among the featured offers on the home page." caption="Tuggi on the BenefitHub storefront." />

## What BenefitHub is

BenefitHub brings together perks from brands in many sectors, such as travel, tickets, beauty and technology. Companies offer the platform to the people who work for them, and each employee picks what fits their own routine.

Being there puts Tuggi in front of people who are already planning their next trip. They are looking for experiences, not only flights and hotels.

## What Tuggi does on your trip

Tuggi is an audio guide that tells the story of the places you pass. You turn the guide on before you leave and put your phone away. When you come near a place worth hearing, the narration starts on its own, with no typing, no stopping the car and no looking at the screen.

Besides the guide, the map shows the points around you, with a name and description in the language of your device. You can also see events happening nearby and ask to be reminded before they start.

## If you found us on BenefitHub

If you find Tuggi through BenefitHub, you get the same app as on the App Store and Google Play. You sign in with your own account. There is no separate version. Everything we describe on this site applies to the app you download.

## Tuggi keeps growing

Tuggi now has more than 2 million mapped points and is present in 65 countries.

Our user base grows month after month. Since January, it has grown by more than 460%. Between July and September alone, it more than doubled.

The product keeps pace. In recent weeks, we explained here how the [guide starts talking on its own](/en/updates/audio-guide-that-starts-on-its-own) and introduced [hour passes](/en/updates/hour-passes).

## Partnerships that take Tuggi further

BenefitHub is one more channel that brings Tuggi to new travellers. It joins our partnerships with businesses that welcome tourists, such as restaurants, hotels, transfers and car rentals. In those places, tourists find a Tuggi QR code and start their trip with the guide in their pocket.

If your company also serves travellers and you would like to discuss a partnership, [see our partner programme](/en/partners) and get in touch.
