Now you buy hours of guide. We stopped selling access by days, and the count works like this: your balance runs while the guide is on, and turning the guide off is what stops it.

The hours you buy never expire. Whatever is left from one trip waits for the next one, so there is nothing to spend in a hurry.

<ArticleNotice body="The change is in the version published in both stores, and it reaches you when you update the app. Anyone who has not updated yet stays on the previous model, and anyone who bought access by days keeps the access they bought, for the term they bought." />

## From days to hours

The unit changed because what uses time is the guide being on. It is the guide that narrates on its own when you pass near a point, and it is the guide that your hours turn on.

## How the hours are counted

Every minute with the guide on uses one minute of your balance. A minute you start and do not finish is not charged, and the count is the same with a signal or without one.

Closing the app does not leave the count running. The session ends with it, and time spent in silence never reaches your balance.

## The passes

There are three sizes, and each one says the kind of trip it was thought for. You choose by how much listening you expect, not by the calendar.

## Before you buy anything

There are welcome hours waiting inside the app, and you are the one who turns them on, once per account. Turning them on costs nothing, because turning them on does not turn on the guide.

Without buying anything you still have the map, the routes and the search. Reading the name and the description of any point is always free, and you can listen to stories by tapping a point, within the day's quota.

## Rather not count hours?

There is also a monthly subscription, for anyone who would rather not watch a balance. It gives unlimited access while it is in effect, it renews automatically every month, and it is cancelled in the store. Its price is on the App Store and on Google Play.

The balance you already bought is not lost while the subscription is in effect: it sits still, it does not expire, and it counts again when the subscription ends.
