The hour passes are in the app. You buy hours of guide, and your balance runs while the guide is on: turning the guide off is what stops it, and every minute on uses one minute of the balance.

The hours you buy never expire. Whatever is left from one trip waits for the next one, and the balance you buy later adds to the one already there.

<ArticleNotice body="The change is in the version published in both stores, and it reaches you when you update the app. Anyone who has not updated yet stays on the previous model, and anyone who bought access by days keeps the access they bought, for the term they bought." />

## What your hours turn on

They turn on the automatic guide, which is the reason all of this exists: it narrates the places you go past on its own, with no typing, no stopping the car and no looking at the screen. The story starts when you come near a place worth hearing — including the place you had no idea was there.

## Discovering costs no hours

The rest of Tuggi asks for no balance. There are more than 2 million mapped points, and Tuggi is present in 48 countries — and all of it opens without buying anything:

- The map, the routes and the search, to find what exists around you.
- Reading the name and the description of any point is always free, in full, in the language of your device.
- Listening to the story of a point by tapping it, within the day's quota.
- Seeing the events happening nearby, opening the card of each one and asking to be reminded before it starts.

And before you buy anything there are welcome hours waiting inside the app: you are the one who turns them on, once per account, and turning them on costs nothing, because turning them on does not turn on the guide.

## How the hours are counted

Every minute with the guide on uses one minute of your balance. A minute you start and do not finish is not charged, and the count is the same with a signal or without one.

Closing the app does not leave the count running. The session ends with it, and time spent in silence never reaches your balance.

## The passes

There are three sizes, and each one says the kind of trip it was thought for. You choose by how much listening you expect.

## Rather not count hours?

There is also a monthly subscription, for anyone who would rather not watch a balance. It gives unlimited access while it is in effect, it renews automatically every month, and it is cancelled in the store. Its price is on the App Store and on Google Play.

The balance you already bought is not lost while the subscription is in effect: it sits still, it does not expire, and it counts again when the subscription ends.
